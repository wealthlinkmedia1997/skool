RankFlow SEO Features

What RankFlow handles for client sites, content, rankings, and reports.

Client onboarding and agency tools

  • Import clients from GoHighLevel or add them through an intake form
  • Kanban pipeline for client stages
  • Three service types: full website, SEO on an existing site, or blogs only
  • GHL onboarding checklist and snapshot loading
  • GHL Onboarding and GHL Cleaner Chrome extensions
  • Agency team roles, plus a client login that sees only its own site

Website building

  • Library of WordPress and Elementor design templates
  • Design changes roll out to every site that uses the design
  • Builds the full site: home, service, location, about, contact, gallery, blog, 404, privacy and terms pages
  • Self-updating WordPress plugin that pushes pages, headers, footers, menus, fonts and colors
  • Protects edits made in WordPress from being overwritten
  • Pulls brand colors from the logo
  • GHL form popups and a Google Map embed

AI content

  • Unique copy for every page and section
  • Duplicate content checker and Uniqueness report
  • FAQs built around real searches so AI tools can quote them
  • Keyword research and grouping with search intent and Excel export

Blogging

  • AI blog posts of 1,500 to 2,500 words with topic research
  • Auto-publishing on a schedule with categories and tags
  • Four branded AI images per post
  • Automatic internal linking with broken-link repair
  • Table of contents and numbered pagination

Images

  • Client photos sync automatically from Google Drive
  • Photos placed on pages automatically
  • AI images as a fallback
  • Duplicate images removed
  • Alt, title, caption and description filled in for SEO

Technical SEO

  • Automatic Rank Math setup, with schema and a focus keyword for each page
  • Full SEO audit and deep crawl, with Scan and Fix tools
  • Speed checks and automatic speed fixes
  • Automatic 301 redirects
  • Robots.txt and crawl optimization

Google Business Profile

  • AI-written GBP posts with rotating keywords, auto-published on a schedule
  • Removes anything Google rejects, like phone numbers and emojis
  • GBP optimization advice
  • GBP photos and keyword list
  • Quick GMB Audit with a Local Dominator report

Reviews

  • Review request campaigns through GHL
  • AI review replies

Rankings and reports

  • Rankings from Google Search Console
  • One-click Search Console setup
  • New blogs sent to Google, Bing and IndexNow automatically
  • White-label client portal with before-and-after site health and a log of work done
  • Scheduled client reports
  • Free audit tool that brings in leads

Growth

  • Managed citations
  • Referral program for agencies
